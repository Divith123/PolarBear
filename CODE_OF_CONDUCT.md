# Code of conduct

PolarBear is a small project made by people who like writing and tools. We want everyone to feel welcome here.

**Be kind and constructive.** Disagree with ideas, not people. Assume good intent, and say clearly what you need.

**Not acceptable:** harassment or discrimination of any kind, insults or personal attacks, sexualised language or
attention, publishing other people's private information, and trolling or deliberately derailing a discussion.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space run by the project. Maintainers may edit,
hide or remove comments, and block people who break these rules.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting (*Security → Report a vulnerability*) or contact a maintainer
listed on the repository page. Reports are kept confidential.

This text is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
